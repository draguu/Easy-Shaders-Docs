Installation

Install Easy Shaders in Blender and choose where your downloaded materials are stored.

Install the addon

  1. Download the Easy Shaders file from Superhive or Gumroad.
  2. In Blender, open Edit → Preferences → Add-ons.
  3. Click Install and select the downloaded file.
  4. Enable the addon. The panel appears in the 3D viewport sidebar (press N).

Set the download directory

Do this first, right after installing. Every material you download is saved to this folder.

  1. Open Edit → Preferences → Add-ons and expand Easy Shaders.
  2. Set the Download Directory to a folder with enough free space.
Tip Choose a drive with plenty of space. High-resolution texture sets add up quickly.

Shaders

Browse more than 1500 materials, download them at the resolution you want, and age them with wear and tear.

Load materials

Press Load Material to load all 1500+ thumbnails. Once loaded, you get a Refresh button, a Categories menu and a Search button.

Browse and search

Browse by category

Select a category, then click any thumbnail to browse the materials inside it.

Search

Press Search and the category menu turns into a search bar. Type anything and the thumbnails update instantly with matching names.

Download a material

  1. Click a thumbnail.
  2. Pick a resolution from the dropdown.
  3. Press Download. The material is saved to the directory you set in Preferences.

Assign a material

When the download finishes, an Assign button appears. Assigning adds a basic material setup to the active object, with all textures connected to a Principled BSDF.

The panel then shows two sections: Basic Controls and Wear and Mixtures.

Basic controls

Use Scale to change the texture scale. Below it are buttons for changing the base properties of the material.

Wear and tear

Open Wear and Mixtures and press Add Wear and Tear. Easy Shaders bakes the maps it needs from your object and prepares it for detail effects. Baking takes a little time, then more options appear.

Scale and Global

Scale
Scale of the wear textures.
Global
Ages the whole object. Move the slider to add more wear.

Effects

Below those you have Grunge, Scratch, Edge, Leaks, Dent, AO and CC. Each has its own settings, available through the small arrow beside it.

Randomise or choose textures

Press Randomise to pick a new texture for each effect, or choose one yourself from the thumbnail dropdown at the far right of the slider. The dropdown is available for Grunge, Scratch, Edge and Leaks.

Grunge

Scale
Scale of the grunge texture.
Color
Switches the grunge between black and white.
Roughness
Adds extra roughness following the grunge texture.

Scratch

Scale
Scale of the scratch texture.
Normal
Adds a slight bump from the scratch texture.
Color
Makes scratches look darker or lighter on the surface.

Edge

Scale
Scale of the edge wear texture.
Color
The color of the edge wear.
Edge Fade
How much of the texture mixes in along the edges of the object.
Roughness
Roughness value of the edge mask.
Bump
Bump value of the edge mask.

Below these is an Edgewear button. On objects where the edge wear map doesn’t generate well, use it to draw the edge wear by hand.

Leaks

Scale
Scale of the leak textures.
Color
Color of the leaks.
Fade
How soft the leaks fade out.
Position
How far the leaks run from top to bottom.

Dent

Dents are generated with a procedural texture.

Scale
Scale of the dent texture.
Color
Color of the dent.
Seed
Changes the dent pattern.
Detail
How much detail the dent has.
Roughness
Roughness of the inside of the dent.
Expand
How large the dent or peeling effect becomes.

AO

Color
Ambient occlusion color.
Value
How much of the surface the occlusion covers.

CC

The main slider changes the hue. Inside you also get Saturation, Value, Brightness and Overlay for basic color correction.

Bake

Fine-tune the edge and AO masks, then bake your finished material into textures.

The Bake tab works on materials that already have Easy Shaders applied. It has two sections: Edge and AO Preparation and Material Baking.

Edge and AO preparation

Adjust the edge and AO map properties used to make the masks. Below the settings you can choose the texture size of the mask and the samples used for baking.

Preview and rebake

  1. Click Preview to see the edge wear on your object.
  2. Change the settings until it looks right.
  3. Press Exit Preview to return to your materials.
  4. Press Rebake. Your existing material is rebaked, reassigned and updated automatically.

Material baking

Set the texture resolution and samples, then choose how the UVs are made:

Auto UV
Smart unwraps your object.
Manual UV
Lets you pick a UV map from the available list.

Then choose what to bake:

Bake Single Object
Bakes the selected material slot only.
Bake All in One
Bakes all available material slots into a single texture.

Press Bake to start. When it finishes, you can switch quickly between the baked material and the original material.

Decals

Place decals on your object, stack them as layers and adjust how they blend with the surface.

Decal library

The Decals tab shows decal categories and a Refresh button to reload the library. Below them is a thumbnail preview where you can check each decal.

Add a decal

Under the preview you have two options:

Add Decal
Enters decal add mode.
Folder icon
Lets you choose your own image and use it as a decal.

In add mode, an empty follows your cursor. Move the cursor over the selected object and the decal is placed according to the face normals.

The panel also shows which material you are working on.

Decal layers

Every decal you add appears in the layer list.

Eye button
Turns the decal on or off.
Up / Down
Moves the decal up or down the list.
Remove
Removes the decal and its parented empty.

Decal properties

Below the list you can set roughness, bump, metallic, color and noise-based texture variation.

Roughness
Roughness of the decal texture.
Metallic
Metallic value of the decal texture.
Bump
Bump of the decal outline.
Texture Bump
Detail bump from the decal texture.
Color Adjustment
Simple color correction for the decal texture.
Color 2
An overlaid color.

Texture variation

Adds noise and a radial gradient around the decal so it blends into the surface.
